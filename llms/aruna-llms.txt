# Aruna

> Aruna is Indonesia's largest integrated fisheries commerce platform, connecting small-scale fishermen to domestic and global markets through technology, a network of processing partners, and distribution hubs (Aruna Hubs) across the Indonesian archipelago. Aruna is a portfolio company of Prosus Ventures. No public API or developer platform is published at this time.
